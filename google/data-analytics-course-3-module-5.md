## 🗣️ Teach it to a friend

Breaking into data isn't just skills — it's people who know you have the skills. You build
that two ways: online (newsletters, LinkedIn hashtags, Kaggle forums, webinars — unlimited by
geography, can reach the whole world) and in person (meetups, conferences, associations —
slower but deeper). The play is both: online for reach, in person for depth. And you don't
need to know anyone to start; you just need to show up where data people already gather.

## 🃏 Flashcards

**Q:** What are the two broad routes for building a data network, and the key advantage of each?
**A:** Online (not limited by where you live — international reach) and in person (easier to have real conversations and exchange information).

**A:** Newsletters (Data Elixir), Kaggle communities/hackathons, LinkedIn/Twitter hashtags, webinars (any three).

**Q:** What's a cheap way to find local data people face-to-face?
**A:** Meetups — search "data science meetups near me"; most are free and many are virtual.

**Q:** Which conference specifically aims to increase underrepresented groups in analytics?
**A:** Women in Analytics.

**Q:** What's DataKind?
**A:** A non-profit promoting ethical data science — offers events and volunteer opportunities for professional growth.
